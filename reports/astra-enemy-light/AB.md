# Self-blind visual comparison

The comparison tool assigned each of twelve enemies an independent random left/right order before judgment. I inspected four pairs per sheet at 984 × 1000 and wrote each sheet's picks and reasons before opening the next. The reveal reads the saved choices. This is one reviewer's visual judgment.

## Phase A

The relit side won 12 of 12. The strongest cues were the warm-to-cool turn across the kettle gauntlet, the weight beneath the Yarn Giant's boots, and the rain rim across the ram's cloak.
