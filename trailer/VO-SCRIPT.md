# Lantern Cat Café: trailer voiceover script

Length **2:00.0** · 14 lines · about 150 words · music at 96 BPM (one bar = 2.5 s). Generated from `trailer/timeline.py`; if a time changes there, run `python3 gen_script.py` again.

## How to record (10 minutes)

1. Quiet room with soft furnishings (a closet full of clothes is ideal). Phone or USB mic 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly off-axis to avoid pops.
2. Record **WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit** if you can (an iPhone Voice Memo .m4a also works). Peaks around −12 dBFS; never clipping.
3. Record **each line as its own file**, named exactly `vo01.wav`, `vo02.wav` … `vo14.wav` (`.m4a` is fine). Do 2–3 takes of each and keep the best; or record one continuous take named `take.wav` with a clear **1-second pause between lines** and the script splits it.
4. Read the line, not the clock. Each line has a **slot** (the longest it may run). Natural pace is about 2.7 words per second; every line below fits that with room to spare.
5. Put the files in `trailer/vo/final/` (or send them to me) and run the two commands in *Inserting your voice*.

Overall voice: a warm storyteller who is a little excited to tell you about this place. Smile on the warm lines, drop lower and slower for the Tangle, whisper the one line in the silence. Never a hard-sell announcer.

## Inserting your voice

```sh
cd trailer
python3 place_vo.py vo/final audio/vo_final.wav     # checks each line fits its slot
python3 mix.py --vo audio/vo_final.wav --out audio/mix_final.wav
python3 assemble.py --reuse --audio audio/mix_final.wav --out out/lantern-cat-cafe-trailer.mp4
```

`place_vo.py` prints each line's length against its slot. A line marked TOO LONG will overlap the next cue: re-record it a touch faster, or nudge its `t0`/`max` in `timeline.py` (the music and picture do not move). The music ducks under your voice automatically; nothing else needs rebuilding.

## Facts behind the economy lines

Every economy claim is something the game actually does, shown with real Fuji transactions (see `CHAIN-FACTS.md`). The script never mentions earnings, prices or returns, and the end card says the tokens are testnet only.
